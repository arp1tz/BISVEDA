BISVEDA - VIDEO DEMO SCRIPT (screen recording + narration + live phone call)
Target length: 3 min 45 s.  About 500 spoken words at a calm pace.
Format per scene:  [time]  SCREEN = what you click/type  |  SAY = what you narrate.

==========================================================================================
BEFORE YOU RECORD (10-minute checklist)
==========================================================================================
1. Start everything with the boot script from C:\BISVEDA:   python scripts/boot.py
   (web on http://localhost:3000, voice bridge on :8001 with the Exotel/ngrok tunnel).
   Open http://localhost:3000/api/helpline/call once and confirm it responds.
2. In prototype/.env set  GEMINI_VISION_MODEL=gemini-3.5-flash-lite  (photos answer in ~5 s).
3. Fresh Groq quota: do not run evals or long test sessions on recording day.
4. Browser: Chrome, 1440x900 window, zoom 100%, bookmarks bar hidden, notifications off.
   Pre-open tab 2 at http://localhost:3000/admin (already logged in).
5. Have ready on the desktop: prototype/public/samples/hallmarked_gold.jpg
6. Phone for the call on speaker, next to the mic, with the ringer ON. Test one call first.
7. Copy the Form I details (Scene 4) to the clipboard so you paste, not type.
8. Do one full dry run. Every query below was tested on 2026-09-29; if any answer looks off
   on the day, ask it once more in a new chat.
9. Record screen and voice separately if you can; it is easier to trim waiting time
   (cut any spinner longer than 2 s in the edit).

==========================================================================================
SCENE 0 - OPENING (0:00 - 0:15)
==========================================================================================
SCREEN: Home page, cursor still. Slowly move over the search box, the + and mic buttons,
        and the "Speak with BISVEDA on Call" pill.
SAY:
"This is BISVEDA, an assistant for Indian Standards and BIS services. Citizens, jewellers and
small manufacturers ask BIS the same questions every day. BISVEDA answers them in their own
language, and every answer shows the exact clause and page it came from."

==========================================================================================
SCENE 1 - CITED ANSWER + HONEST REFUSAL (0:15 - 0:55)
==========================================================================================
SCREEN: Type:  I make battery-operated toy cars for kids. Which standards apply?
        While it works, point at the progress steps.
SAY (while loading):
"I'm a toy maker. Watch the steps: it understands the question, searches the official BIS
documents, checks the evidence, then writes."
SCREEN: Answer appears. Click the first [S1] pill. The source drawer opens. Hover "Open PDF".
SAY:
"It names IS 15644 for electric toys and IS 9873 for mechanical safety. Each claim has a source
tag. Click it and you see the exact clause and page from the standard. Nothing is written
from memory."
SCREEN: Close the drawer. Type:  domestic pressure cooker stainless steel standard
SAY:
"And when the documents we hold don't cover something, it says so and sends you to the
official BIS catalogue instead of guessing."

==========================================================================================
SCENE 2 - HALLMARKING: PHOTO CHECK + CENTRE FINDER (0:55 - 1:40)
==========================================================================================
SCREEN: Click "+ New chat". Click +, attach hallmarked_gold.jpg, type:
        Is this hallmarked? What does the mark mean?
SAY (while loading):
"Now a consumer with a gold ring. I just upload a photo."
SCREEN: Point at the photo card (22K916, AB12C3), then the hallmark card, then the cited line.
SAY:
"It reads the marks from the photo: 916, which IS 1417 defines as 22 carat, and the HUID code.
It checks the HUID format, cites the five marks a hallmarked piece must carry, and sends you to
the BIS Care app to verify it. It never declares jewellery genuine on its own."
SCREEN: Type:  Show hallmarking centres in Jaipur
SAY:
"Need a centre nearby? These are operative hallmarking centres from the official Manak Online
list of more than sixteen hundred, on a map."

==========================================================================================
SCENE 3 - HINGLISH (1:40 - 2:00)
==========================================================================================
SCREEN: Type it, or click the mic and say it if the mic worked in your dry run
        (speech-to-text may write Hindi in Devanagari; the answer is still Hindi, just in a different script):
        sone ke gehno par 916 ka matlab kya hai aur hallmark mein kya kya hota hai
        When the answer appears, click the read-aloud (speaker) button for 3 seconds, then stop.
SAY:
"People don't always type in English. Here I ask in Hinglish, by voice. The answer comes back in
Hinglish, with the same source tags, and it can read the answer aloud."

==========================================================================================
SCENE 4 - FORM I: JEWELLER REGISTRATION, CHAT TO WORD FILE (2:00 - 2:45)
==========================================================================================
SCREEN: "+ New chat". Type:  mujhe apni jewellery shop ka hallmarking registration karna hai
SAY:
"A jeweller wants to register for hallmarking. BISVEDA asks for everything Form I needs, in one
list."
SCREEN: Paste:
        Shop ka naam Shree Ganesh Jewellers hai, address 14 MG Road, Wardha, Maharashtra, phone 9876543210.
        Proprietorship hai, owner Utkarsh Kale (Proprietor). Sirf sone ka certificate chahiye.
        Turnover 2.5 crore hai, koi cancellation nahi hua, hamara mark SGJ hai,
        contact person Utkarsh Kale proprietor 9876543210
SAY:
"I answer in one message, in my own words. It fills the form and cross-checks it. Here it
noticed the address has no PIN code."
SCREEN: Type:  PIN code 442001 hai   ->  "Draft ready". Click "Download Word draft (.docx)",
        open the file in Word for 3 seconds.
SAY:
"Done. I get an editable Word draft in the official Form I layout, ready to check and submit on
Manak Online."

==========================================================================================
SCENE 5 - PHONE CALL (2:45 - 3:20)
==========================================================================================
SCREEN: Back in the chat, click "Talk on Call" in the header. Keep "Start new" selected.
        Type your number, click "Call me now". Show the "Call request queued" screen.
SAY:
"Not everyone has a smartphone or wants to type. Anyone can request a call."
[Phone rings. Answer on speaker. Let the greeting play (about 5 s).]
YOU ASK ON THE PHONE (Hindi):   "Motorcycle helmet ke liye kaunsa standard hai?"
[Agent answers: IS 4151, ISI mark.]
YOU ASK:   "Sone ka HUID kaise check karu?"
[Agent answers: BIS Care app, Verify HUID.]   Say "Dhanyavaad" and hang up.
SAY (over the end of the call, or in the edit):
"The same assistant, by phone, in Hindi, Marathi or English."
DO NOT ask the phone agent how many marks a hallmark has, or about cement, water or steel
pipes: the phone agent does not yet read from the same cited documents as the web chat.

==========================================================================================
SCENE 6 - FOR BIS STAFF (3:20 - 3:40)
==========================================================================================
SCREEN: Switch to tab 2 (/admin). Knowledge Graph: search IS 1417, click the node (2 s).
        Documents tab, IS 1417, "Version Control & Amendments" (3 s).
        Directory tab, hallmarking centres table (2 s).
SAY:
"For BIS staff there is a console: every standard, clause, lab and hallmarking centre linked in
one graph, the edition and amendments each answer is based on, and the official lab and centre
directories with their source and date."

==========================================================================================
SCENE 7 - CLOSE (3:40 - 3:50)
==========================================================================================
SCREEN: Back to the home page.
SAY:
"BISVEDA: cited answers, in your language, by text, photo, voice or phone call. When the
documents don't support an answer, it tells you where to go instead."

==========================================================================================
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG WHILE RECORDING
==========================================================================================
- Answer is short or looks off: re-ask once, or cut it in the edit.
- Photo takes longer than 10 s: cut the waiting in the edit; check GEMINI_VISION_MODEL.
- Call does not ring in 20 s: check the boot script window for the voice bridge and ngrok lines,
  then retry. As a last resort record the call separately and splice it in.
- A "429" or very generic answer across the board: the Groq daily quota is used up; record the
  next day.
